## Reading order for this module

If you are working through mod-101 for the first time, read in this order:

1. NIST AI RMF 1.0 (AI 100-1) executive summary + Playbook landing page (chapter 01).
2. NIST GenAI Profile AI 600-1 §2 (the twelve risks) — chapter 02.
3. ISO/IEC 42001 outline and ISO/IEC 23894 outline (freely available from ISO), plus a public summary of ISO/IEC 42005 — chapter 03.
4. EU AI Act Articles 9, 10, 14, 15, 55, 61, 72 in the consolidated OJ text — chapter 04.
5. SR 11-7, OCC 2011-12, GMLP guiding principles, PCCP final guidance, and Colorado SB 24-205 — chapter 05.
6. Chapter 06 needs no external reading — you already have the ladder.

## Reading order — the paywalled standards

ISO/IEC 42001, 23894, 42005, 25059 are paywalled through ISO national bodies and resellers. Options:

- Buy from your ISO national body (in the US: ANSI web store). Institutional access may be available through your employer.
- Use lead-implementer / lead-auditor training courses, which include licensed standard copies as part of curriculum.
- Rely on publicly available *summaries and outlines* published by ISO on the standard's landing page — these give clause identifiers and titles without the full requirement text.

Do not rely on unofficial re-postings of standard text; those are copyright violations and their fidelity to the current edition is uncertain.
