# Labs — mod-101 AI Risk Frameworks and the Risk-Engineer Position

Short, guided, hands-on sessions. These are *drills* that make the exercises' larger deliverables feel routine; they do not duplicate the exercises. Work through them in order after you have read the matching chapter.

| Lab | Chapter(s) covered | Duration | What you build |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`lab-01-five-sentence-sub-category-translation.md`](lab-01-five-sentence-sub-category-translation.md) | Chapter 01 | 45 min | Four NIST AI RMF sub-category five-sentence reads (says / means / test / pass / evidence) for a fixed toy system (`PolicyPal`, internal HR-policy RAG). |
| [`lab-02-one-artifact-many-frameworks.md`](lab-02-one-artifact-many-frameworks.md) | Chapters 01, 03, 04, 05 | 60 min | A single cross-walk file that files one LLM red-team report against NIST RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, ISO/IEC 25059, EU AI Act Article 15, and SR 11-7 — training the chapter-03 "same evidence, many standards" reflex. |
| [`lab-03-ladder-triage-drill.md`](lab-03-ladder-triage-drill.md) | Chapters 03, 05, 06 | 40 min | A triage of ten inbox items into owned / consumed / handed-off / escalated / out-of-scope buckets, with a role-boundary-risks footnote. Trains the *runtime* behaviour of the chapter-06 deferral contract. |

Labs assume you have read the referenced chapters. They do *not* require any paid standards access or infrastructure beyond a terminal and a markdown editor.
