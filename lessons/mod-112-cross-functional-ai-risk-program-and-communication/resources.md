## Reading order for this module

If you are working through mod-112 for the first time:

1. Re-read mod-101 ch 06 (deferral contract) and mod-102 ch 04 (frontier-lab shape templates) — both are load-bearing prerequisites for this module's chapters.
2. NIST AI RMF Playbook — MEASURE and MANAGE sections — before chapter 05.
3. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (via licensed access) — Annex A controls and clauses 4-10 — before chapter 05. If licensed access is not immediate, the ISO standard landing pages have publicly available scope summaries.
4. Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness, Google DeepMind FSF — the current published versions — before chapter 04.
5. Model Cards for Model Reporting (Mitchell et al.) and Datasheets for Datasets (Gebru et al.) before chapter 03. The Hugging Face model-card documentation is the operational format to work in.
6. IAPP AIGP body of knowledge, ForHumanity criteria library, BABL AI curriculum, and the ISO/IEC 42001 LI / LA training-body catalogues — enough to make an informed choice — before chapter 06.

## Reading order — paywalled and versioned sources

- ISO/IEC 42001, 42005, 23894, 25059 are paywalled through ISO national bodies. Same options as prior modules: national-body purchase, lead-implementer / lead-auditor training, or the publicly available scope summaries on ISO's standard landing pages.
- Frontier-lab documents (RSP, Preparedness, FSF) are versioned; pin the version you cite and note the publication date. Prior versions remain instructive as evolution studies.
- Certification-body materials (IAPP AIGP body of knowledge; ForHumanity criteria; BABL AI curriculum; ISO/IEC 42001 LI / LA course content) are versioned and often available only to enrolled learners or paying members. The published outlines on each body's website are the free-tier reference.
- Regulator guidance (SR letters, FDA guidance documents, EU AI Act delegated acts, Colorado AI Act rules, NYC LL 144 rules) is published in the official channels linked above and superseded by amendment; always cite the version-of-record.

Do not rely on unofficial re-postings of any standard, certification-body curriculum, or frontier-lab document; those are copyright violations for paywalled standards and their fidelity to the current release is uncertain.
