# Labs — mod-102 Harm Modelling and Risk Taxonomy Engineering

Short, guided, hands-on drills. These make the module's exercises' larger deliverables feel routine; they do not duplicate the exercises. Work through them in order after you have read the matching chapter.

| Lab | Chapter(s) covered | Duration | What you build |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`lab-01-harm-chain-drill.md`](lab-01-harm-chain-drill.md) | Chapter 01 | 45 min | Three immediate → secondary → cascading → systemic harm chains on a fixed toy system (`PolicyPal`, internal HR-policy RAG), each anchored to a distinct chapter-01 misuse persona and capability surface, with a first-pass cause-bucket hint per scenario. |
| [`lab-02-cause-bucket-attribution-drill.md`](lab-02-cause-bucket-attribution-drill.md) | Chapter 05 (with chapter 01 / 06 support) | 40 min | Eight pre-written one-line harm scenarios triaged across the operator / model / data / integration buckets using the "what one change ends this scenario?" test, with two full chapter-05 mitigation-contract blocks and a `id_group`-split row for a compound scenario. |
| [`lab-03-evidence-trace-drill.md`](lab-03-evidence-trace-drill.md) | Chapter 02 | 45 min | One full `evidence_refs` cell for a fixed harm-model row — AIID + OECD.AI AIM + MIT AI Risk Repository + literature — with analogy strength per citation, a reverse-sweep coverage-gap candidate list, a `confidence` decision, and a watch-trigger block. |

Labs use `PolicyPal` as a fixed toy system so the drill output is small, checkable, and easy to compare to the shape expected by the chapters. They do *not* require any paid standards access; they do require a browser for the public incident / risk corpora in lab 03.
