# Labs — mod-103 Risk Quantification, Scoring, and Enterprise AI Risk Registers

Short, guided, hands-on drills. These make the module's exercises' larger deliverables feel routine; they do not duplicate the exercises. Work through them in order after you have read the matching chapter.

| Lab | Chapter(s) covered | Duration | What you build |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`lab-01-three-state-scoring-drill.md`](lab-01-three-state-scoring-drill.md) | Chapter 01 | 45 min | A complete three-state (inherent / residual / control-defeated) scoring table for a fixed row (`HR-PP-RTV-01` on `PolicyPal`), with both deltas (inherent − residual, worst control-defeated − residual), two named bypass modes, monitor refs, appetite reads, and a one-paragraph audit narrative. Drills the atomic skill exercise 01 asks for at scale. |
| [`lab-02-axis-rubric-and-anchor-drill.md`](lab-02-axis-rubric-and-anchor-drill.md) | Chapter 02 | 45 min | Impact / likelihood / exposure scored on the chapter-02 working rubric for the same fixed row, each axis citing a verbatim rubric-level phrase, an anchor-evidence type from the chapter-02 table, and a one-sentence argument. Combined level derived under a stated combination rule, with the chapter-02 anti-pattern self-check run against it. |
| [`lab-03-calibration-report-consumption-drill.md`](lab-03-calibration-report-consumption-drill.md) | Chapter 07 | 45 min | Three calibration-report decisions (well-formed, borderline, disqualifying) for the same fixed row: a chapter-07 provenance block and a calibration-acceptance record on the accepted report; two calibration-request blocks on the refused reports; portfolio-aggregation treatment of the uncalibrated case. Drills the L25 ↔ L30 seam and the five chapter-07 anti-patterns. |

Labs use `PolicyPal` and `HR-PP-RTV-01` as the fixed toy system / row — carried forward from mod-101 and mod-102 labs — so each drill's output is small, checkable, and easy to compare against the shape expected by the chapters. They do *not* require any paid standards access, infrastructure, or external web access beyond a terminal and a markdown editor.
