# Quizzes for mod-111-incident-response-and-postmortems (AI-Specific Incident Response and Post-Mortem Engineering)

Knowledge-check quizzes for the module. Open-book — the chapters are the reference. Each question tests understanding of a concept rather than recall of wording, and the answer key cites the chapter section the answer comes from.

- [`quiz-01-ai-incident-response-and-postmortems.md`](quiz-01-ai-incident-response-and-postmortems.md) — twelve multiple-choice questions covering all six chapters: chapter 01 on the seven-class AI-specific incident taxonomy, the harm-shape severity rubric (single-party serious harm can be SEV1; confirmed regulator-reportable events are always at least SEV1), and the three-axis routing decision (product IR / security IR / governance IR with "conditional" as a documented decision, not a default); chapter 02 on the six evidence surfaces (trace store, model version, prompt / retriever / decoding config, guardrail-effectiveness delta, drift signals, upstream / supply-chain change log), the six-step "what changed since the incident began" workflow, the cause-vs-symptom ranking discipline, and the two-sentence converged causal-chain paragraph; chapter 03 on the ten-section post-mortem shape that carries the load for three audiences (engineering / governance council / regulator) and the CAP discipline (named individuals, due dates, evidence-of-completion artefacts); chapter 04 on the pinned regression fixture (six required fields, two-destination landing in red-team + app-eval suites, pre-mitigation red before post-mitigation green); chapter 05 on the regulator-facing extract pattern (one post-mortem as source of truth, declarative extracts as views, CI-style consistency checks, EU AI Act Article 73 15-day envelope, SR 11-7 MRM memo shape, FDA MDR 30-day / 5-working-day envelope, HIPAA 60-day with GDPR Article 33 72-hour concurrency); chapter 06 on the eight-stage incident-response loop, the SecOps / DFIR boundary that this role does *not* cross, and the level-40 escalation to `agentic-safety-engineer` for frontier-agent incidents. Take the quiz after reading all six chapters; the exercises and the labs land better once the shape questions are internalised.
