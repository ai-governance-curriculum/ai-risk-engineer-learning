# Labs for mod-111-incident-response-and-postmortems (AI-Specific Incident Response and Post-Mortem Engineering)

Three 45-minute guided drills. Each builds one atomic output-shape authoring skill the exercises assume, on a fixed running system (`HelpDesk`, a customer-support RAG assistant for a mid-size B2B SaaS company; in-house fine-tune `helpdesk-finetune` v1.3.0 on the vendor-hosted base `FoundationCo-LLM-3.5` v3.5.2; EU-and-US deployment on a US-bank sponsoring organisation; the same `HelpDesk` carried through the mod-109 and mod-110 labs). The labs do not duplicate the module's exercises — the exercises run against the reader's own primary running system (full triage of a ten-candidate mixed queue in exercise 01; full six-step RCA workflow with seven deliverables in exercise 02; full ten-section post-mortem with three audience-specific extracts in exercise 03; full eight-step fixture back-feed with AIID / AIM compare-point search and register-credit wiring in exercise 04; three regulator-facing extracts with cross-package consistency check in exercise 05) using the module's running incident `INC-2026-08-14-cs-rag-inj-01`. The labs drill the output shapes those exercises rely on against the fixed `HelpDesk` system with fabricated-but-plausible identifiers and metadata, using deliberately distinct fabricated incidents (`INC-2026-11-21-hd-qual-01`, `INC-2026-11-13-hd-pii-01`, and a two-candidate triage queue dated 2026-11-20 / 2026-11-24) so the lab artefacts and the exercise artefacts are not confused.

- [`lab-01-triage-workbook-row-authoring-drill.md`](lab-01-triage-workbook-row-authoring-drill.md) — 45 minutes. Author the chapter-01 triage-workbook row shape end-to-end for two fabricated candidates on `HelpDesk`: candidate L-01 (a `tenant_tier` fairness-slice parity move after a v1.4.0 rollout, deliberately shaped to force the harm-shape SEV1 reading on a 100%-available service plus the three-axis routing of biased-outcome escalation — Yes / No / Yes + legal) and candidate L-02 (a many-shot staging exploit that emits a span resembling a training-corpus record, deliberately shaped to force the pending-class state between membership-inference and hallucination-driven harm plus the "conditional with a written trigger" security-IR cell plus the register-row gap-list shape). Includes the ten-column workbook row shape, the conditional-trigger log with flip-to-Yes and flip-to-No evidence per block, the harm-model / register gap list with proposed mod-102 / mod-103 edits, the SEV1-only reportability signal naming the clock-start timestamp and the accountable submitting role per obligation, and the frontier-agent-escalation negative check against chapter 06. Chapter 01 skill feeding exercise 01's ten-candidate triage queue.
- [`lab-02-causal-chain-paragraph-authoring-drill.md`](lab-02-causal-chain-paragraph-authoring-drill.md) — 45 minutes. Author the chapter-02 step-4 explanatory-power ranking and the step-6 two-sentence converged causal-chain paragraph for a pre-computed six-surface delta record on `HelpDesk` incident `INC-2026-11-21-hd-qual-01` (a quality regression where a tool-descriptor edit on `escalate_ticket` at 03:47Z caused a planner refusal-default on nominal invoice-lookup paths; the output-distribution drift and tool-call-distribution drift are *symptoms* of the tool-descriptor edit, not independent causes; the model surface and the guardrail surface show no diff). The lab provides the pre-computed window and trace IDs, the six-surface delta YAML, the paired-good-trace layered diff, and the replay record — the drill is the ranking (with at least one delta labelled as a symptom of another delta higher up) and the two-sentence paragraph that names the delta, the mechanism, the observed effect, and the guardrail interaction, plus the contributing-factors separation and the chapter-02 anti-pattern self-check. The paragraph is the artefact that post-mortem section 4 lifts verbatim and that the Article 73 extract's "preliminary root cause" field quotes. Chapter 02 skill feeding exercise 02's full six-step workflow.
- [`lab-03-pinned-fixture-yaml-authoring-drill.md`](lab-03-pinned-fixture-yaml-authoring-drill.md) — 45 minutes. Author the chapter-04 pinned-fixture YAML for the PII-egress incident `INC-2026-11-13-hd-pii-01` on `HelpDesk` (indirect prompt injection through the mirrored help-forum corpus induces a `verify_account_email` tool call permitted by a `verify_*`-glob allow-list misconfiguration) covering every one of the six required fields (verbatim prompt; retrieval chunk with `content_sha256`; model `base` / `fine_tune` / adapter with manifest hash; decoding config with pinned seed; allow-list before / after and guardrail versions post-mitigation; expected pass / fail with positive concrete criteria — guardrail verdicts, allowed / forbidden tool-call sets, output-pattern required-one-of and forbidden), plus the two derived cross-references (`incident_ref`, `register_row`) and the two-role sign-off provenance block. Lands the byte-identical fixture at both the red-team-suite and the app-eval-suite paths and confirms the two copies hash-match (chapter 04 §"Both destinations"; the parallel-suite-drift anti-pattern). Authors the pre-mitigation RED and post-mitigation GREEN verification record with the specific failing / passing checks named per run and both runs cited for CAP-4 evidence-of-completion. Closes with the anti-pattern self-check against the four chapter-04 anti-patterns (the "we'll add it later" fixture; the bloated fixture; the manual-verification fixture; the parallel-suite drift). Chapter 04 skill feeding exercise 04's full back-feed workflow.

Run the labs in order — they each exercise a distinct fabricated incident on the shared `HelpDesk` system, but the shape skills stack: lab 01's triage-row shape is the entry point of the loop; lab 02's causal-chain paragraph is what the post-mortem's section 4 consumes; lab 03's fixture YAML is what the CAP's fixture-back-feed action produces. Together the three drills carry the atomic output-shape authoring skills that exercises 01, 02, 03, and 04 depend on, so when the exercises run on the reader's own real incident the time goes into engineering judgement — which candidates are AI-specific on a noisy queue, which delta is causal vs. symptomatic on a real six-surface diff, which positive concrete pass criteria actually catch the next regression on a real suite — not into inventing the artefact format. Chapter 03's ten-section post-mortem shape and chapter 05's three-obligation extract-generation shape are exercised through the quiz and through exercises 03 and 05 directly rather than through dedicated labs, because the engineering judgement in both chapters is the *content* authored across the ten sections (or across the Article 73 / SR 11-7 / HIPAA / GDPR packages) against the reader's real incident, not the file layout — and the lab-level forcing function is covered by the labs' drilling of the artefacts those documents lift (the causal-chain paragraph at lab 02; the pinned fixture at lab 03; the triage row at lab 01). Chapter 06's loop-and-boundaries content is the module's closing synthesis and is exercised through the quiz and through the stage-owner artefacts produced across exercises 01-05.
