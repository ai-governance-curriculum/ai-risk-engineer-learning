# Labs for mod-107-privacy-risk-engineering (Privacy-Risk Engineering for ML and LLM Systems)

Three short guided drills. Each builds one atomic report-shape authoring skill the exercises assume, on the same running `CareTriage` example (a gradient-boosted tabular classifier used for care-coordination follow-up prioritisation at a regional care-coordination organisation; Article-9 cohort in the training data; black-box API returning top-1 label plus the two-class probability vector). The labs do not duplicate the module's exercises — the exercises run the real tools (ML Privacy Meter, Opacus / TF Privacy, Presidio) against the reader's own running system; the labs drill the output shapes those artefacts rely on against the fixed `CareTriage` system with fabricated-but-plausible numbers.

- [`lab-01-mia-slice-report-authoring-drill.md`](lab-01-mia-slice-report-authoring-drill.md) — 45 minutes. Author the chapter-02 MIA slice-report end-to-end on fabricated Shokri + LiRA numbers against `CareTriage`: threat-model header, Shokri and LiRA on the same per-slice table (overall / Article-9 cohort / outlier-decile / small-class), AUC alongside TPR @ 1 % FPR and @ 0.1 % FPR on every row, the AUC-can-lie interpretive paragraph, a parameter-conditioned residual-risk claim, and the register-row handoff on `REG-CT-MIA-01` with inherent / two control-defeated cells. Chapter 02 skill feeding exercise 01 and lab 02's residual-column overwrite.
- [`lab-02-dp-sgd-tradeoff-and-defence-memo-drill.md`](lab-02-dp-sgd-tradeoff-and-defence-memo-drill.md) — 45 minutes. Author the chapter-04 utility / privacy trade-off table across a non-private baseline and two DP-SGD variants (ε = 8 and ε = 2) on `CareTriage`, the three-grounds operating-point defence memo (threat-model calibration, utility calibration, comparability to published references including US Census 2020 DAS), and the six-failure-modes checklist (Poisson sampling, `ModuleValidator.fix`, gradient accumulation, warm-start from non-DP checkpoint, data-dependent hyperparameter selection, non-DP eval on training data) — plus the register-row residual overwrite on `REG-CT-MIA-01`. Chapter 04 skill feeding exercise 04 and the chapter-06 report's Section 4.
- [`lab-03-gdpr-mapping-and-boundary-notes-drill.md`](lab-03-gdpr-mapping-and-boundary-notes-drill.md) — 45 minutes. Author Section 7 (GDPR mapping with Article 22 as the anchor) and Section 10 (open items and boundary notes) of the chapter-06 privacy-risk report on `CareTriage`, inheriting the lab-01 and lab-02 numbers: the five-row GDPR mapping table, the Article 22 engineering-read paragraph that forecloses the legal conclusion, the DPIA note that routes trigger decisions to DPO + L15, the parameter-conditioned residual-risk claim that forecloses the "GDPR-compliant" anti-pattern, three counsel-routed items (Art. 22 applicability; Art. 6 secondary-purpose lawful basis; Art. 35 DPIA trigger) with fact pattern + evidence + escalation on each, three L30-routed items (CIs on LiRA TPR; statistical-significance framing of slice-level deltas; distribution-shift robustness of the eval), and three risk-council items. Chapter 06 skill feeding the final privacy-risk report authoring at the end of the module.

Run them in order — lab 02 inherits the baseline LiRA number from lab 01, and lab 03 inherits the register-row cells and the operating-point defence from labs 01 and 02. The three labs together drill the atomic shape-authoring skills that exercises 01 and 04 (and, by composition, the full privacy-risk report at the end of the module) depend on, so the exercises' time goes into engineering judgement on the reader's own running system, not into inventing the output format.
