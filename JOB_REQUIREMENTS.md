## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical task domains for an AI Risk Engineer (level 25, AI Governance family) from the public references catalogued in `.aicg/job-requirements.json → authoritative_references`.
2. Mapped each task domain to (a) the role on our level ladder that should own it primarily and (b) the curriculum module that will cover it.
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — assign coverage to the lowest-level role that genuinely requires the skill; higher-level tracks link back rather than duplicate. In particular, deferred:
   - *Down* to `ai-governance-analyst` (level 15) for the analyst legwork this role consumes — AI-system-inventory maintenance, jurisdictional regulatory tracking, first-draft impact-assessment authoring, framework-crosswalk legwork, and reading eval / audit evidence into governance records.
   - *Down* to `ml-engineer` (level 20) for classical ML / PyTorch / sklearn / MLflow / packaging fundamentals; to `ai-infra-junior-engineer` (level 10) for engineering-craft prerequisites.
   - *Sideways* to `ai-eval-engineer` (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family) for application-layer LLM/agent evaluation-engineering depth (traces, judges, RAG eval, eval-gated CI/CD, online eval).
   - *Sideways* to `model-evaluation-engineer` (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family) for benchmark, statistical, and judge-vs-human calibration methodology depth.
   - *Sideways* to `ai-infra-mlops` (peer, level 25, Infra family) for MLOps pipeline / registry integration.
   - *Sideways* to `security-learning` / `ai-infra-security` (peer / next-up, level 35) for deep ML/AI security depth at platform scale (model-extraction defence at the edge, judge supply-chain, eval-set exfiltration prevention, MLSecOps).
   - *Up* to `ai-evaluation-engineer` (peer / next-up, level 35, Governance family) for release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth.
   - *Up* to `agentic-safety-engineer` (level 40) for frontier-agent red-team methodology and dangerous-capability evaluation depth.
   - *Up* to `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50) for control-library architecture, policy taxonomy, and cross-jurisdiction reconciliation; to `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60) and `chief-ai-officer` (level 70) for program leadership, board-level reporting, and regulator engagement.
   - *Out of scope* to legal counsel (this role does not deliver legal opinion) and to security operations / DFIR (SOC-side incident handling; this role feeds AI-specific signal to SecOps).
4. Flagged everything that has not yet been validated against in-window postings so the next cycle can demote any requirement whose evidence stays empty.

## Ownership map — quick reference for next cycle

When backfilling postings, use this ownership decision to keep the curriculum from drifting into peer territory:

- **AI Governance Analyst** (level 15, `ai-governance-analyst-learning`) — owns operational analyst work: intake, inventory, framework crosswalks, first-draft impact assessments, model-card / datasheet analyst-tier authoring, control-tracking, jurisdictional tracking, reading eval / red-team evidence and filing it. **This risk engineer consumes those analyst artifacts and adds engineering depth on top.**
- **AI Risk Engineer** (this track, level 25, Governance family) — owns the *engineering craft* of AI risk: harm-model authoring, risk quantification, adversarial-ML and LLM red-team engineering, fairness / privacy / guardrail engineering, monitoring-into-risk-register integration, MRM / supply-chain assurance, incident RCA, cross-functional program at team scope.
- **AI Eval Engineer** (peer, level 30, AI Engineering family, `ai-eval-engineer-learning`) — owns application-layer evaluation-engineering depth (traces, judges, RAG eval, eval-gated CI/CD, online eval, cost / latency / quality). **This risk engineer hands app-side safety measurement and guardrail effectiveness rubric to that peer.**
- **Model Evaluation Engineer** (peer, level 30, ML Engineering family, `model-evaluation-engineer-learning`) — owns benchmark, statistical, and judge-vs-human calibration methodology depth. **This risk engineer uses that methodology on its own risk claims.**
- **AI Infra MLOps** (peer, level 25, Infra family, `ai-infra-mlops-learning`) — owns MLOps automation. **This risk engineer authors the risk-gated release contract; that peer implements the pipeline.**
- **Security / AI Infra Security** (peer / next-up, level 35, `security-learning` / `ai-infra-security-learning`) — owns deep ML/AI security depth at platform scale. **This risk engineer covers adversarial-ML at working depth; platform-scale depth is linked out.**
- **AI Evaluation Engineer** (peer / next-up, level 35, Governance family, `ai-evaluation-engineer-learning`) — owns release-assurance / audit-trail / regulator-facing methodology depth. **This risk engineer produces the risk-engineering evidence that peer packages.**
